Display & sharing

Saved instantly on change. Each post has two share buttons: tall (▯, a fixed phone-friendly card) and wide (▭, the magazine layout at the width set here). Reading column width is the desktop reader measure (reloads take effect on the reader). Unread pills still count awaiting posts, so a feed can look busy while its stream stays quiet. Colour scheme follows your browser by default; some installed (PWA) windows report their own theme rather than the system's, so you can pin light or dark here.

    Fetching politely

    These save instantly. Sites block readers that fetch too fast: a new feed can mean dozens of article pages at once. Requests to the same site are spaced out, and when a site answers "403 forbidden" or "429 too many requests" the feed pauses (doubling on repeats, up to a day) and its posts wait in the queue instead of failing. Some sites only serve real browsers: "Use this browser's" copies the User-Agent of the browser you're using right now.

        Maintenance

        Retention deletes articles older than the window (all statuses) and their now-orphaned images. The cache cap trims oldest files first. Runs automatically every 6 hours and once at startup.

        Danger zone

        Permanently deletes every post that failed to digest, plus any cached images only they used. Use it after fixing the cause (model down, bad prompt) - new posts from the same feeds will be fetched again on the next poll. This cannot be undone.
